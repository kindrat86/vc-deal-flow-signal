Day six. The Data Nerd. Sixty seconds.

This is the audio companion to the twelve-minute walkthrough on YouTube. If you only have time for one of the two right now, listen to this — it's the load-bearing two minutes of the longer version.

I want to show you what the dashboard does in the ninety seconds before a Sunday morning becomes a Sunday afternoon. You open the digest email. Five names. You scan for the sector that matches your thesis. You click the org. You read the most-changed repo. You read the last ten commits. You write a three-line outbound to the founder with one specific observation about the codebase.

That entire loop, end to end, takes between four and seven minutes. The reply rate when you lead with a code observation is roughly four times the reply rate when you lead with a deck-question. I have run this loop one hundred and seventy-three times in the last year. Reply rate is eighteen percent. Conversion to a real conversation is six percent.

Six percent of one hundred and seventy-three is ten conversations a year that you would not have otherwise had. From a Sunday morning ritual that costs you fifteen minutes and zero euros.
