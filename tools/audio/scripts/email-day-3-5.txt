Day three and a half. The Data Nerd. Ninety seconds.

This is the underlying shift in plain language. Russell Brunson calls it the Conversion Story. Five sentences. Read in order.

One. The old way you were sold. The best deals come from your network. Build the rolodex. Every operator-turned-investor is told this on day one of their first fellowship.

Two. The new vehicle. Engineering acceleration. Public, reproducible, code-side. Every great startup leaves a footprint in its code twenty-one to forty-seven days before the deck circulates. Methodology is on SSRN. Cost is nine ninety-seven a month.

Three. External struggle, removed. You don't need partner-grade tooling. Harmonic and Tracxn are a thousand a month because they serve fund-grade procurement. Pull the sales motion out and the same data ladder runs at nine ninety-seven. The category was priced wrong for you, not built wrong.

Four. Internal struggle, removed. You don't need to become someone else to source. The data path lets you stay the engineer who reads commit logs for fun. Identity stays intact. The signal does the introduction.

Five. The frameworks. Sunday digest. Wednesday filter. End-of-quarter sweep. Twelve minutes a week. Methodology published. The shift is wired into the product — you're not buying a tool, you're buying a cadence.

If steps one through four read like the room you're standing in, step five is the door.
