## Dream Customer (defined before scoring)

**The dream customer is an angel investor or small-fund manager** who writes €5k–€50k checks, wants to be first in the room (not third shown the deck), and is tired of warm-intro networks that show deals a week after they've already closed. They speak VC/startup language, use GitHub, but may not read code themselves. The site segments them explicitly: angel (€5k-€50k), fund/syndicate lead, operator-who-writes-checks, curious.

## The 5 Things That Move the Needle (ordered by impact)

### 1. Turn on the Dream 100 engine (Traffic Secrets — lifts 44→60, +16 pts on Traffic)
The /target-list page documents 100 voices. The /distribution/platform-hooks page has 12 platform-native openers written. **Ship the outreach.** Pick 5 targets from the Dream 100 list, send one personalized opener per day for 10 days, document which got replies. This is the single highest-ROI action — the infrastructure is built but the faucet is off.

### 2. Add real customer stories (Expert Secrets — lifts 55→75 on Social Proof)
0 of 25 charter seats claimed. The site has no testimonials, no case studies, no named users. Get the first 3 charter members, interview them, publish "How [Name] used Code-Side Sourcing to write the first check on [Startup]." Even one real story lifts social proof from credential-only to peer-validated.

### 3. Add the missing sensory detail to the Epiphany Bridge low point (Expert Secrets — lifts 72→82 on Epiphany Bridge)
The Saturday scene is good but abstract. Add: the car model outside, the exact cold coffee on the desk, the specific WhatsApp group name, the false belief you were holding ("I told myself it was geography"). One paragraph of sensory detail and a named false belief pushes this from 72 to 82+.

### 4. Build a Soap Opera email sequence (DotCom Secrets — lifts 50→75 on Follow-Up)
The free digest is great for Seinfeld but there's no 5-email Soap Opera for new subscribers. Write and schedule: Email 1 — The Saturday I missed the round (story), Email 2 — Why every other deal source is a lagging indicator (education), Email 3 — The 3 objections breakdown (belief shift), Email 4 — How to read your first signal in 30 seconds (quick win), Email 5 — The offer (soft pitch). Set up in Resend, trigger on subscribe.

### 5. Launch one paid traffic test (Traffic Secrets — lifts 20→35 on Bought Traffic)
The site has comparison pages built to capture competitor-brand search traffic (Harmonic.ai, Crunchbase, PitchBook). Launch a €500 Google Ads campaign targeting those competitor brand names + "deal flow tools for angel investors." The landing pages are already optimized for these searches — you're leaving money on the table by not paying for position 1 on competitor terms.

## Strengths to Protect

- **The 10-rung value ladder** is the best I've seen on any Brunson audit. Free → €0.99 → €1 → €7 → €9.97/mo → €97/mo → €497/mo → €1,997 → €14,997/yr → €49,997/yr. Every rung has a "when this fits" explanation. This is Two Comma Club architecture.
- **The hook specificity** ("47 days before the deck arrives") with a named research paper (SSRN, n=219) backing the number is world-class. Most founders say "earlier than anyone else" — you say "47 days" and cite the panel.
- **The objection-handling section** (three objections, three breakdowns) is textbook Brunson. Head-on, respectful, evidence-backed.
- **Agent-native distribution** (MCP server, A2A endpoint, OpenAPI spec, /md mirrors, Chrome extensions) is ahead of 99% of SaaS products. When AI agents become the primary discovery layer, you've already built the on-ramps.
