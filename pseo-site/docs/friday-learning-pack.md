## Friday decision rules

### Rule 1 — choose the page that earned curiosity
A page is a winner if it did at least one of these:
- gained impressions
- gained clicks
- triggered a reply
- triggered a follow-up question
- produced a second click into the next page

If more than one page qualifies, pick the page that moved closest to money or proof.

### Rule 2 — choose the page that leaked trust
A page needs patching next week if it did one of these:
- people asked “what is this?” after seeing it
- people asked for methodology because the page didn’t explain enough
- people asked for proof because the claim felt unsupported
- people clicked it but did not continue deeper
- it got distributed several times and produced nothing

### Rule 3 — stop doing what produced no signal
Stop or pause a page-angle-platform combination if:
- it got 3+ real touches
- in good contexts
- with clean snippets
- and still produced no response, no clicks, no next-step interest

Do not keep feeding a dead combination because it sounds smart.

## How to read GSC data

Look for:
- impressions rising with no clicks -> snippet / title / query fit issue
- clicks rising but no deeper movement -> handoff issue
- no impressions at all -> authority / distribution issue
- impressions on the wrong page -> internal-link or query-matching issue

## Strong week signals

Good week:
- `/research` gets cited or requested
- `/compare/...crunchbase...` gets tool-difference replies
- `/weekly/top-100` gets “what does this score mean?” replies
- `/from-stars-to-seed` gets proof-led follow-up
- `/methodology` or `/receipts` gets real second-step use

Weak week:
- all traffic stays direct only
- no second-step pages get used
- top pages get attention but no follow-up questions
- long-tail inventory gets crawled but commercial/proof pages do not move
