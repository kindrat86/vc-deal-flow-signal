Dataset Details

Aggregate engineering momentum benchmarks broken down by industry sector. Each sector entry includes total startups tracked, average commit velocity (14-day rolling window), average contributor count, and overall sector activity ranking. Data derived from the Q3 2026 VC Deal Flow Signal dataset of 324+ venture-backed startups. Sectors ranked by startup count: Web3 (48), EdTech (34), E-commerce Infrastructure (27), Data Infrastructure (25), Healthcare (24), Supply Chain (23), Enterprise SaaS (21), Social & Community (19), Space Tech (18), Robotics (17), Gaming (17), PropTech (16), Legal Tech (13), HR Tech (11), AgriTech (11). Updated weekly.
