Dataset Details

Daily ideation corpus generated from VC Deal Flow Signal's tracked startup data. Each entry takes a real startup's engineering signal (commit velocity change, contributor growth, signal type) and reframes it as an actionable SaaS opportunity. Includes headline, idea thesis, build angles (3 per entry), and a distribution play strategy. 87 entries covering 16 sectors across 85+ unique startups. Signal types represented: framework migration (60 entries), engineering hiring burst (11), infrastructure buildout (9), deploy frequency spike (7). Published daily from April 23 to July 19, 2026.
