How do VCs use Signals to screen deals?

Answer: VCs use Signals to triage inbound deal flow — scoring hundreds of startups automatically to surface the top 5-10% worth a partner meeting, saving 40+ hours per week on initial screening.

The typical VC receives 200-500 pitch decks per month. Signals automates the first-pass filter: each startup gets scored, ranked, and bucketed into priority tiers. Partners review only the top tier, with full signal breakdowns explaining why each startup scored high.
