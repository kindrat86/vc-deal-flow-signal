I've been studying what GitHub repos look like in the 14 days before a startup announces a raise.

The pattern is surprisingly consistent:

Before: steady, predictable commit rhythm. Maybe 3 to 5 contributors pushing code at a regular cadence.

Then: a sharp break. Velocity doubles or triples. New contributors appear (often senior engineers or specialists). New repos get created, usually infrastructure, CI/CD, or platform-level.

The company is either deploying fresh capital into engineering, or building furiously to hit a milestone that will trigger a raise.

Either way, the 14-day acceleration window is the single best leading indicator I've found.
