Quick question: what sources do you use for deal flow right now?

If you're like most angel investors, the list looks something like:

- Warm intros from other investors
- AngelList / syndicate platforms
- Twitter/X and LinkedIn
- Demo days and pitch events
- Accelerator batch announcements

All good sources. All lagging indicators.

By the time a startup shows up in any of those channels, they've already decided to raise. The narrative is polished. The deck is ready. Multiple investors are looking.

GitHub activity is the one source that shows you what's happening before the founder decides to fundraise. A team shipping faster doesn't lie, and it doesn't wait for a PR strategy.

That's the blind spot. GitDealFlow fills it — it's a tool, not a fund, so it works for you instead of competing for the deal. It surfaces the startups heating up early in your sectors; you just look.
