## Slide copy
1. Why hiring pages are weaker than filled roles.

2. A job page can be stale.
It may outlive the role, the hiring plan, or the team.

3. A job page can be duplicated.
One role can appear in several places without a filled seat.

4. A job page can be unfilled.
An open listing is not a headcount change.

5. Use hiring pages as a prompt for a question.
Then look for other public evidence.

6. Public data helps form a diligence list.
