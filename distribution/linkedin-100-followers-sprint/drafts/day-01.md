## Company post text
A percentage spike is only a starting point. First ask what the baseline was, whether contributors changed, and whether the work reached a release.

Public GitHub activity can make a diligence list sharper. It does not prove a fundraise, revenue, or product-market fit.

## Slide copy
1. A 3,275% GitHub spike can mean almost nothing.

2. Percentages hide the denominator.
A move from 1 to 34 commits is real activity, not a verdict.

3. Cross-check contributor count.
One person pushing harder is different from a growing engineering surface.

4. Check new repositories and releases.
Work that reaches a release is easier to investigate than a raw commit burst.

5. Add external context.
Public engineering activity is one research input, alongside your own diligence.

6. Use the spike to choose what to investigate.
Not what to predict.
