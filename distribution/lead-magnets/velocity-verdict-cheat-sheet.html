The three-signal screen

One metric can lie. A coordinated change is harder to dismiss.

SIGNAL 01

Velocity surge

Is the team shipping more than its own recent normal? Read the delta, not the lifetime total or a single busy day.

SIGNAL 02

Contributor breadth

Is the work spreading across more people? A broad team change carries more information than one noisy maintainer.

SIGNAL 03

Infrastructure buildout

Are new repos, services, or release surfaces appearing? Look for systems that support a larger product or customer load.

Slow down when you see:

×One account created most of the change.
×The spike is docs, formatting, vendored code, or bot traffic.
×Activity lasted one day and immediately returned to baseline.
×The company is fully closed-source or the public repo is not its core work.
