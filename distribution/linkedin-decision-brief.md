# LinkedIn decision brief — unblock the "LinkedIn strategy (TBD)" item

**Status:** BLOCKED since 2026-04-15 on one decision: use personal LinkedIn or not.
**This brief exists so the decision takes 5 minutes, not another two months.**

## The constraint that decides it

`AGENTS.md` anonymity policy: *"Maintainers are pseudonymous. Do not surface a maintainer's
real name in any generated output, README, package metadata, or external listing."*

A personal LinkedIn profile posting GitDealFlow content **breaks the brand's core design
pillar**. The Data Nerd persona, the @data_nerd account, the pseudonymous SSRN setup — all
of it unwinds the moment a real name + face is attached. This is not reversible later.

## Options

### A. Personal profile posts GitDealFlow content — REJECT
Breaks anonymity permanently. Also: your personal network (Omilia colleagues, Upwork
clients) is not the buyer. Low reach into VC LinkedIn without years of presence there.

### B. GitDealFlow company page, no person attached — VIABLE, LOW PRIORITY
- Cost: ~1h setup + content repurposed from the daily tweets (same numbers, longer form).
- Reality check: company pages without employee amplification get near-zero organic reach.
  Expect single-digit impressions for months.
- Verdict: do it only as an SEO/legitimacy artifact (a company page makes the brand look
  real when VCs google it), not as a distribution channel. 1 post/week, fully automatable.

### C. Skip LinkedIn entirely — DEFENSIBLE DEFAULT
The buyer (seed VCs) is reachable on Twitter/X, in newsletters, and via the dataset itself.
LinkedIn adds a channel where the brand is weakest (no person, no network).

## Recommendation

**C now, B later.** Close the blocked item as "no personal LinkedIn — anonymity policy."
Create the company page only after the first paying customer, as a legitimacy artifact.
Spend the reclaimed time on the Reddit/Twitter cadence where the pseudonymous persona
already has an account and a voice.

The daily "linkedin engagement brief" automation (commits of 06-04..06-08) was generating
briefs for a channel we never decided to use — recommend pointing that agent at Twitter
engagement instead.
