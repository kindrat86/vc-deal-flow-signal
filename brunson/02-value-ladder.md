# Brunson Workbook 2: Value Ladder (Speed Run)
## vc-deal-flow-signal | 2026-04-13

### Pricing Tiers (use in Stripe Payment Links + landing page pricing block)

- **Tier 1 (Free):** Monthly email digest with top 5 trending startups by GitHub momentum. Lead magnet, no login required.
- **Tier 2 (Pro, EUR 9.97/mo):** Full micro-SaaS dashboard. Browse and filter 50+ startups ranked by engineering acceleration. Filter by sector, stage, geography. Enriched with funding data and team size. Monthly subscription.
- **Tier 3 (Insider Circle, EUR 97/mo):** Everything in Pro, plus custom watchlists (monitor specific companies or sectors), API access, Slack alerts for real-time spikes, deeper enrichment (tech stack, key hires, repo-level detail), portfolio overlap detection, and quarterly trend briefings. Monthly subscription.

### Funnel Type for Day One
Lead funnel. Rationale: free monthly email digest captures investor emails at zero friction. Dashboard upsell happens inside the digest ("See 45 more startups and filter by your sectors: upgrade to Pro"). This matches the angel investor buyer: they browse deal flow passively, so a push-based lead magnet converts better than asking them to sign up for a tool cold.
