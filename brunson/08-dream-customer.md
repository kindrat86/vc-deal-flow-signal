## 1. Dream Customer Profile (the avatar)

**Name:** Alex, the Developer-Investor.

**Stage of life:** 32 to 45. Senior/staff engineer, ex-founder, or current technical partner/scout. Has an exit or a senior-eng paycheck funding their angel activity. Already invested in 5 to 40 companies.

**A day in their life:**
- 07:30 — Checks Hacker News over coffee. Upvotes a Show HN.
- 08:30 — Opens Cursor / Claude Desktop / Warp. Shipping their own code OR reading diligence notes on a company in their pipeline.
- 10:00 — Scrolls Twitter feed: Swyx, Simon Willison, Arvid Kahl, Levelsio, Packy, Gergely Orosz, Elad Gil, Avichal.
- 12:00 — Lunchtime scroll through an AngelList syndicate invite. Skim-reads the deck. Asks "what's their GitHub look like?" and manually browses the repo.
- 15:00 — Cursor Discord #showcase. Claude Discord. r/ClaudeAI.
- 18:00 — Writes a check OR ships a feature. Rarely both.
- 22:00 — Reads a Pragmatic Engineer issue in bed.

**Biggest pain:**
- Deal flow from Twitter is the same as everyone else's Twitter. It's consensus before they can act.
- Manually reading GitHub repos per company does not scale past 10 companies.
- Paid institutional tools (Harmonic, Dealroom, PitchBook) are $10k+ and built for partners at funds with analysts, not for a solo operator.
- Wants to find the company while it is still a GitHub org with 12 contributors, not after the TechCrunch post.

**Dream outcome:**
- Open Claude, type "which AI infra startups are accelerating this week?", get an answer with real velocity numbers in three seconds.
- One email on Sunday that lists the five highest-signal startups nobody else is tracking yet.
- Reach out to a founder Thursday morning and be the first investor in their inbox.

**False beliefs to break:**
1. "GitHub data is too noisy." Addressed: we show regime change, not raw commits.
2. "I already have deal flow." Addressed: your deal flow is consensus deal flow; signal is pre-consensus.
3. "I'm not a quant." Addressed: ranked list, human-readable, no math required.
4. "If it's only €9.97, it can't be serious." New false belief for the Developer-Investor tier. Addressed: the price is low because there's no enterprise sales team. The data is identical to what a $10k/yr tool would deliver. Bootstrapped founder pricing for a bootstrapped-ethic buyer.

## 3. Congregation Strategy (who does what)

**Work in mode, play in mode:**

Every day Alex logs into Cursor, Claude, GitHub, Twitter, HN, one Discord. GitDealFlow needs to appear in at least two of those surfaces without paying a cent. MCP server (Claude/Cursor) + HN front page + Twitter reply presence = covers the "work" side of their day. Free Signal Digest + Telegram channel = covers the "scroll" side.

**Hooks that fit the Developer-Investor specifically:**

1. **MCP server = the product.** Distribution and product-market fit in one surface. When Alex asks Claude "any interesting fintech startups this week," the MCP answers. That is the wedge.
2. **"Claude and Cursor natively" as the headline feature.** Traditional competitors (Harmonic, Dealroom) cannot do this. It is the one thing you have that none of them will ever build.
3. **Data transparency over data exclusivity.** Institutional tools sell proprietary black boxes. Alex distrusts black boxes. Your methodology doc is a feature, not a footnote.
4. **Price as a signal of independence.** €9.97 is not an apology. It is a statement: "I do not have an enterprise sales team and I am not going to charge you for one." Frame it that way.
