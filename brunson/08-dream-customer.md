## 8.1 Marcus at a glance

| Trait | Detail (from `brand/voice.md`) |
|---|---|
| Who | **Marcus** — a corporate-development / corp-dev-adjacent professional at a mid-to-large tech acquirer. Also reads as a **PE operating partner** or a **non-engineer tech VP**. |
| Job | Evaluates companies for **partnership, acquisition, or investment**. |
| Critical constraint | **He does not read code himself.** His technical diligence depends on borrowed engineering time. |
| What he needs | Engineering signal **translated into business language** — who's accelerating, who's stalling, who's worth a meeting — *not* raw commit data. |
| Anonymity | No public name/face (per anonymity policy). |

## 8.2 Pains, desires, fears (for hook/offer copy)

- **Pains:** drowning in inbound; can't tell real traction from hype; gets
  burned trusting decks; his technical diligence depends on borrowed eng time.
- **Desires:** a credible shortlist he can act on; to look smart in front of his
  deal team; to move before competitors; plain-English rationale.
- **Fears:** backing/acquiring a dud; missing the obvious winner; looking
  non-technical in a technical room.

## 8.3 What this means for targeting (the big one)

Marcus is **not** a developer. **Reach him via translated, business-language
signal surfaces — not dev channels.** This single line reorders the entire
Dream 100: the people who hold Marcus's attention are business-of-tech analysts,
SaaS/cloud-metrics and GTM voices, PE/M&A/holdco operators, tech-dealmaking
journalists, and the market-map / category-analyst accounts he uses to build a
shortlist. OSS maintainers, infra founders, and "investors who code" are the
*wrong* list for Marcus — that was the deprecated developer-investor avatar.

## 8.4 Where he can be reached

Business-language surfaces. On X specifically: the strategist/analyst,
SaaS-metrics, PE/M&A, and tech-deals-media accounts in the Dream 100. Outreach
must obey `marketing/messaging-guide.md` (lead with the *job* — a credible,
plain-English shortlist — never with raw code or quant jargon; "a tool, not a
fund"; translate the signal, don't dump it).
