Every week, a handful of startups quietly double their engineering velocity. New contributors, new repos, accelerating commits. ## Story (use in About section + email 1)
I kept losing deals to investors who somehow got there first. Same networks, same events, same AngelList alerts. Then I noticed a pattern: the startups that broke out always showed an engineering spike weeks before anything public happened. More commits, more contributors, new infrastructure repos. It was all right there on GitHub, in the open, and nobody was watching. So I built a system that watches. Now I see the acceleration before the pitch deck lands.
