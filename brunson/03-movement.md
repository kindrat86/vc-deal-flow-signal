# Brunson Workbook 3: Creating Your Movement (Speed Run)
## vc-deal-flow-signal | 2026-04-13

## Attractive Character (use in About section + email voice)
**The Data Nerd.** Obsessive about signals others ignore. Speaks in patterns, numbers, and "here's what I found." Not flashy, not guru-ish. Shows the data, explains why it matters, lets the reader draw conclusions. Tone: curious, precise, slightly contrarian. Shares GitHub findings the way a quant shares alpha: matter-of-fact, with receipts.

Voice rules:
- Lead with data or a surprising observation, never with hype
- Use specific numbers ("commit velocity up 340% in 14 days") instead of vague claims
- Admit uncertainty when the signal is ambiguous
- Never pitch, always show

## New Opportunity Positioning (use in hero + first email)
**Old way:** Paying for intent data from vendors who sell the same list to everyone. Every investor gets the same "hot companies" report. By the time you see it, it's not alpha, it's noise.

**New opportunity:** GitHub is the largest free dataset of real-time engineering activity on the planet. Nobody packages it for investors. VC Deal Flow Signal reads the commit logs, contributor graphs, and repo patterns that predict breakout companies, weeks before any pitch deck, press release, or AngelList trending badge.

**Opportunity switch statement:** You don't need better deal flow sources. You need to read the signal that's already public and that nobody else is watching.

## Future-Based Cause (use in manifesto block on landing page)
We believe the next generation of great investments will be found in data, not networks. The best startups leave footprints in their code long before they leave footprints in the press. Our mission is to make engineering momentum visible to every investor, not just the ones with the right connections. A world where capital finds builders faster is a world where better products get built.
