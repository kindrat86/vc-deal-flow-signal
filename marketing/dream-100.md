## Working the Dream 100: Weekly cadence

**Daily (10 min):**
- Reply to 3-5 Tier 1/2 Twitter accounts with a data insight
- Like/retweet 5 relevant investor posts

**Weekly (60 min):**
- Post 1 original Data Nerd thread on Twitter (from pSEO data)
- Post 1 LinkedIn article (repurposed from Twitter thread)
- Comment on 2 Indie Hackers / Reddit threads with a signal example
- Engage in 1 Slack community with a useful data point

**Monthly (2-3 hours):**
- Pitch 2 newsletters from list A for guest contribution
- Pitch 1 podcast from list B for guest appearance
- Review and update Dream 100 list (who responded, who didn't)
