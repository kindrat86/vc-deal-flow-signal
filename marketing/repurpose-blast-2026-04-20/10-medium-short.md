**The backstory**

Infisical entered the secrets management market at the right time. HashiCorp Vault, which had been the go-to for enterprise secrets management, was acquired by IBM/Broadcom in 2023. Enterprise customers restless with that acquisition have been shopping for alternatives.

Doppler, the VC-backed competitor that raised $20M in 2021, has been quiet. The developer-first, open-source alternative with enterprise-grade reliability is essentially unsold.

Infisical has the GitHub presence (tens of thousands of stars), the contributor base, and apparently this week, the engineering intensity to take that market.

**What infrastructure buildout looks like**

In our signal taxonomy, "infrastructure buildout" isn't just "lots of commits." It's a qualitative classification based on commit patterns: new services being scaffolded, dependency graphs being restructured, configuration management being overhauled.

These are not bug fixes. They are foundations being laid for something larger.

At post-seed stage, this pattern is meaningful. Early-stage teams don't rebuild infrastructure casually. It requires engineering time, focus, and usually a clear reason — a new enterprise customer requirement, a new product tier, or preparation for a fundraising round where demo quality matters.
