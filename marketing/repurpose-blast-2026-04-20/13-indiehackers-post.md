This week's most interesting data point from running GitDealFlow:

Infisical (open-source secrets management) spiked +1496% commit velocity over 14 days. 399 commits from 100 contributors. Signal type: infrastructure buildout.

That pattern in our dataset is the most reliable precursor to a fundraising announcement. The historical lag is 3-6 weeks from when we detect the spike to when it shows up in press.

Context that makes this particularly interesting: HashiCorp Vault was acquired by IBM, which unsettled its developer community. Doppler (the VC-backed alternative) has been quiet since 2021. The open-source, developer-first secrets management market has a real gap. Infisical is positioned to take it.

We've been tracking 4,200+ startup GitHub orgs for six months and this is one of the clearest infrastructure buildout signals we've seen.
