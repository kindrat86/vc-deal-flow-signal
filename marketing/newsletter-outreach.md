## Key rules for guest posts
- Lead with value, not product pitch
- Include real data with specific numbers
- Be honest about limitations ("the signal doesn't tell you why, just that something changed")
- CTA should be soft: "If you want to see this data weekly, I publish a free digest at gitdealflow.com"
- Never ask to mention pricing or the paid product in the guest piece
