# Dream 100 (X-only) — Built for Marcus
## Built: 2026-05-30 | Framework: Russell Brunson, *Traffic Secrets* | Avatar: `brand/voice.md` §Avatar (canonical) + `brunson/08-dream-customer.md`

The Dream 100 is the list of accounts that already hold the attention of our
dream customer. **Marcus is a corp-dev / PE-operating-partner / non-engineer
tech-VP buyer who does NOT read code** and needs engineering signal *translated
into business language*. Per the canonical avatar: **reach him via
business-language surfaces, not dev channels.**

So this list is built **the opposite way** from a developer Dream 100. No OSS
maintainers, no "investors who code," no infra-founder dev Twitter — Marcus
isn't there. Instead: the strategists who translate tech into business, the
SaaS/cloud-metrics voices, the PE/M&A/holdco operators, the tech-dealmaking
press, and the market-map / category-analyst accounts Marcus uses to decide
*who's worth a meeting*.

**Posture rules (from `messaging-guide.md` + anonymity policy):**
- We show up as the **product/data**, no founder face/voice/name.
- Every touch is **translated, business-framed**: "who's accelerating / stalling
  / worth a meeting," plain-English rationale — never raw commits or quant jargon.
- "A tool, not a fund." We hand Marcus a credible shortlist; he makes the call.

**Tiers:**
- **T1 — lean in now:** the analyst/metrics/PE/market-map voices whose audience
  *is* corp-dev / PE / non-eng-VP buyers (Buckets A, B, D, F).
- **T2 — sustaining cadence:** VC "who's hot" commentary (C) and deals-media (E)
  — high reach, looser audience match.

> ⚠️ **Handle accuracy:** the corp-dev / PE / business-tech crowd is a more
> concentrated niche on X than dev Twitter, and several brand/firm handles below
> are best-guesses flagged `(confirm)`. **Verify every handle before outreach**
> and before loading into `tools/dream100-mention-radar/`. I prioritized
> audience-fit and honest flagging over padding to a round 100 with shaky handles.

## Working Marcus's Dream 100: cadence

**Daily (10 min):**
- 3–5 replies on Bucket A/B/D posts — each carries one **translated** data point
  ("Company X's engineering activity is accelerating faster than its category —
  worth a look," + link). Never raw commits, never quant jargon.

**Weekly (45 min):**
- 1 business-framed "who's accelerating this week" post/thread pairing our signal
  with a recognized benchmark (Clouded Judgement / Meritech comps style).
- 1 outreach to a Bucket E/F account offering a *complementary, translated* data
  angle (the data desk / market-map play).

**Monthly:**
- Load confirmed handles into `tools/dream100-mention-radar/` to catch when any
  of the 100 discuss deal-sourcing, traction-vs-hype, or our surfaces.
- One translated, business-language note to a warmed Bucket A/D voice — lead with
  a credible shortlist insight, per `messaging-guide.md`. This is the path to Marcus.
