## Russell's Verdict (in his voice)

*"OK, four passes in, here's what I'm seeing. You finally fixed the close
stack — Money, Identity, Pricing, Urgency, plus the encore — that's textbook
Expert Secrets. Big Domino's loud on three surfaces, your fail story is real,
your stack slide is on price. I'd run that webinar.*

*What's still missing — and it's the same gap on three different chapters
across two books — is the Dream 100 and the distribution map. You're doing
the work, you're showing up on Reddit and dev.to and Substack and Mastodon and
Bluesky and Farcaster, but the visitor can't see it. Brunson rule: every
hidden funnel asset is a closed funnel. If your reader can't see where you
live on the internet, you're paying the rent on a ghost.*

*Same for the funnel-hack frame — Section 3 of DotCom is "reverse-engineer
the funnels that already work." You're doing competitive teardowns at
`/buyers-guide` but you're not teaching the frame. That's the difference
between selling a tool and selling a methodology.*

*And — this is small but real — you don't have a 5-minute Perfect Webinar.
Your 12-minute one is great for the buyer who's ready to read. The 5-minute
exists for the buyer who isn't.*

*Fix Dream 100, fix the distribution map, fix the 23 building blocks frame
on `/funnels`, fix the Conversion Story script on `/perfect-webinar`, and
you're at 92."*
