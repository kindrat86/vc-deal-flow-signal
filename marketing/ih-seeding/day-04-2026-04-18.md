## Comment 2

**Link:** https://www.indiehackers.com/post/people-don-t-use-this-when-they-get-a-contract-they-use-it-right-before-signing-36bcf0edd3

```
The usage-timing gap is everywhere in SaaS data.

Tools pitched for the full workflow get used at the last decision point. Contract review. Code review. Invoice review. Humans only reach for assist right before they commit.

Pricing the urgency step works better than pricing the process.
```
