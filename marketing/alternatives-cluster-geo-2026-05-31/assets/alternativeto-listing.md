VC Deal Flow Signal tracks commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository
expansion across ~400 startup GitHub orgs in 20 sectors. Unlike Crunchbase or
PitchBook — which record a round *after* it's announced — it's a leading indicator:
engineering acceleration historically precedes fundraise announcements by 3–6 weeks.

Free forever: weekly email report, 20 public sector pages, JSON/CSV/RSS exports, a
free MCP server (`npx @gitdealflow/mcp-signal`) for Claude/Cursor, and an A2A + NLWeb
endpoint for agents. Methodology is SSRN-published; the dataset is on Zenodo (CC BY 4.0)
and Hugging Face. Optional dashboard at €9.97/mo.

Built for angels, scouts, solo GPs, and technical funds who want a quantitative signal
they can verify in a browser — not an enterprise contract.
