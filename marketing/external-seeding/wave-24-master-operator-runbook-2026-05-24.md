========================================
7. Send / don’t send decision
========================================

Use:
- `wave-18-send-dont-send-decision-tree-2026-05-24.md`

Send only if
- there is real context
- you know the exact question
- you can answer in one sentence
- you know the exact page

Do not send if
- context is weak
- homepage is your default answer
- you need 3 links to explain yourself
- you are reacting emotionally
