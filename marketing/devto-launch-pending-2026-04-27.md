```
Six months ago I started tracking GitHub activity for 4,200 startup orgs that ended up raising seed rounds.

Five patterns kept showing up about three weeks before the announcement. Star counts were not one of them. The leading signals lived inside the repo: commit velocity spikes from 5 commits/week to 50+, the contributor count crossing 5 unique authors, README rewrites, dependency-graph sprawl past 200 packages, and a very specific kind of issue burst tied to a single new feature surface.

I wrote up the full pattern, the misses, and the part I got wrong:
```
