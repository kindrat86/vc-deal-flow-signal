## Stylistic notes

Each draft varies on:
- Opening hook (compliment / paper reference / paper read / their work direction).
- Body cadence (one short interrupted sentence / one long flowing / one pivot / one continuous).
- Sign-off (uniform, but the line above it varies in commitment level).

What's deliberately NOT in any of these:
- "I hope this finds you well" / pleasantries
- "I am the author of..." (anonymity per Option A)
- Any link to gitdealflow.com (only SSRN)
- Calls to action / asks for citation
- Marketing language about the product, dashboard, pricing, MCP server, etc.
- Long paragraphs

## What to do if a recipient replies

- **Generic positive ("thanks, will take a look"):** acknowledge briefly, don't push for more.
- **Methodological question:** answer it factually, link to relevant section of the SSRN paper.
- **Citation interest:** offer the BibTeX entry from `signals.gitdealflow.com/research/citations.bib` (this URL is fine to share once they ask).
- **Hostile / "this is spam":** apologize once, do not re-email, log in `send-log.md`.

## Halt conditions

If any of the first 2 sends produces a hostile reply, bounce, or spam complaint, the remaining 2 sends in the queue are cancelled and the campaign is paused for review.
