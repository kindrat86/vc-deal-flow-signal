### 3. BREAK FALSE BELIEFS (12 min) — The Teaching Section

This is where you deliver real value. Three "secrets," each breaking one false belief from Brunson Workbook 4.

#### Secret 1: "GitHub data is too noisy" (Vehicle belief)
- **Teach:** Show the difference between raw commit counts (noisy) and acceleration patterns (signal)
- **Live demo:** Pull up a real startup's GitHub. Show the 90-day baseline. Show the spike. Explain what "regime change" looks like.
- **Stat:** "In our dataset, companies showing a 2x contributor spike within 14 days have repeatedly preceded fundraise announcements or major product launches within 60 days — consistently enough to be a leading indicator."

#### Secret 2: "My network gives me enough deal flow" (Internal belief)
- **Teach:** Explain the "consensus trap." When 5 investors are looking at the same deck, terms get worse.
- **Live example:** Show a startup from your data that hasn't appeared on AngelList trending or in any newsletter, but is showing strong acceleration.
- **Reframe:** "Your network shows you what others already see. This shows you what nobody else is watching."

#### Secret 3: "Public data can't give me an edge" (External belief)
- **Teach:** The quant fund analogy. SEC filings are public too. The edge is in the analysis layer.
- **Live example:** Show the pSEO sector ranking page. Point to a specific company. Say: "This data is all public. But before today, you didn't know to look at it this way."
- **Stat:** "Zero investor tools currently package GitHub activity as a deal flow signal."
