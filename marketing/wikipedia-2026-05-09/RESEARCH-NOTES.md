## What NOT to claim in the Wikipedia drafts

The following claims appear in the project's marketing copy but should NOT appear in any Wikipedia draft, because they cannot be sourced to a third party:

- "21 to 47 days before pitch decks circulate" — too specific, sourced only to the project.
- "Reference implementation of code-side sourcing" — neologism that hasn't entered third-party usage.
- "6-12 weeks pre-fundraise" — specific timing claims need third-party validation.
- Specific company names (Y Combinator batch examples, etc.) — Wikipedia requires cited examples, not project examples.
- Anything about pricing, tiers, or commercial offerings.

The Wikipedia drafts use the more general phrasing "approximately three to six weeks" / "several weeks", which is in the SSRN paper's abstract and is therefore citable.
