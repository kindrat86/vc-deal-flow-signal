Crunchbase Pricing: Plans, Cost, and What You Get in 2026

Crunchbase has a free tier plus three paid plans. Pro costs $49 per user per month billed annually ($588 per year) or $99 per month billed monthly. Business starts around $199 per user per month, and Enterprise and API access are custom-quoted. Add-ons for verified contact data run $29 to $99 per month.

What Each Crunchbase Plan Includes

The free tier gives you basic company search and limited profile views, enough to check a startup's funding history but not to run a sourcing operation. Starter adds more search depth for light prospecting. Pro is the workhorse for an individual researcher: advanced filters, AI-powered summaries, saved searches, alerts, a tracker board, and about 2,000 export rows per month. Business layers on CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot), prediction signals, and about 5,000 export rows per month for teams. Enterprise and API tiers unlock programmatic access and bulk licensing, and are priced per seat on custom quotes.

What Actually Drives Crunchbase's Cost

Three things push the bill up: seat count (every plan is per user per month, so a 5-person team multiplies fast), export limits (2,000 rows on Pro versus 5,000 on Business), and add-ons. Verified contact data, which is what most prospecting teams actually want, is a separate add-on running $29 to $99 per month on top of the base plan. A 5-person team on Pro with contact data can easily land at $5,000 to $10,000 or more per year before any enterprise negotiation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Crunchbase have a free plan?

Yes. Crunchbase has a free tier with limited search, a small number of saved searches, and basic company profiles. Paid tiers unlock advanced filters, export, alerts, and CRM integrations.

Is Crunchbase Pro $49 or $99 per month?

Both are correct at different billing cadences. Crunchbase Pro is $49 per user per month billed annually ($588 per year), or $99 per month billed monthly.

Does Crunchbase offer a free trial?

Yes, Crunchbase Pro has a free trial (typically 7 days). Enterprise and API access are custom-quoted and generally require a sales conversation.
