GitDealFlow for Solo GPs

Why Solo GPs Use GitDealFlow

Solo GPs face the same competition as multi-partner firms but with a fraction of the resources. There is no analyst bench to screen the market, no dedicated sourcing associate to maintain coverage maps, and no data team to build proprietary tools. The result is that independent managers often end up depending on inbound deal flow, which is, by definition, the flow every other fund has already seen.

GitDealFlow is built for that asymmetry. It reads the public GitHub activity of 350+ startups and tracks commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository expansion, the signals that tend to precede fundraising by 21-47 days. A solo GP who watches those signals gets institutional-grade sourcing: a weekly list of five names, every Sunday, each verifiable on GitHub in minutes.

The pattern behind the product was documented in a research panel of 219 fundraises, and the methodology is published on SSRN under a CC BY 4.0 license with an open dataset. That matters for a solo GP: you are staking your track record on sourcing decisions, and the evidence behind them should be checkable, not a black box.

How GitDealFlow Works for You

Every Sunday you receive five names by email, selected for accelerating engineering momentum. Your job is the part only you can do: pick the one or two that fit your thesis, verify them on GitHub, and reach out early, while the founders are still assembling the round, 21-47 days before the news breaks.

  • Build proprietary deal flow without hiring an analyst or buying enterprise data contracts.
  • Use sector filters and momentum tracking to stay focused on your thesis, not the market's.
  • Verify every signal yourself on GitHub before it enters your pipeline.
  • Pair the digest with the free public API and MCP server on signals.gitdealflow.com for AI-assisted research.

Getting Started

Sign up for the free Signal Digest. Review the five names each Sunday, verify the strongest candidates on GitHub, and keep a simple log of what you saw and when. Within a few weeks you will have a pipeline built from evidence, and a record of being early that you can show LPs. The digest unsubscribes in one click; paid tiers (Dashboard and Insider) add deeper tracking when your volume demands it.

Why This Audience Uses Code-Side Signals

GitDealFlow for Solo GPs cares about one question: which teams are accelerating before the market notices. The GitDealFlow dataset answers it from public engineering activity: 350+ startup GitHub organizations, 15 sectors, refreshed weekly, with breakout teams surfacing 21 to 47 days before their round is announced. The signal slots into existing workflows rather than replacing them: it prioritizes which warm intros to chase, which database alerts to trust, and which portfolio companies are quietly decelerating.
