How is this different from the affiliate program?

The affiliate program is for individuals who refer investors and earn 30% recurring. It is one-directional: you send traffic, we handle everything else.

A JV partnership is bidirectional. We co-create assets with you (reports, landing pages, integration pages), both brands appear on the deliverable, and the revenue share is higher (35-40% vs 30%). JVs are for organizations with an audience, not just individuals with a network.
