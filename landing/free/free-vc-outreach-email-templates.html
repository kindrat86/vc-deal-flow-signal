FAQ

What response rate should I expect?

Cold outreach: 5-15% response rate. Warm intros: 30-50%. Personalized emails referencing specific traction (like GitDealFlow momentum) get 2-3x the response of generic outreach.

How long should outreach emails be?

Under 100 words. VCs read emails on mobile between meetings. Get to the point in the first sentence.

Should I send a deck in the first email?

No. Ask for permission first. 'Would a deck be useful?' gets better responses than attaching a deck cold.
