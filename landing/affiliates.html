Frequently asked questions

When and how do I get paid?

Commission is paid manually via bank transfer or PayPal once the customer's payment has cleared. For monthly subscriptions, you are paid monthly. For one-time purchases (Sector Sweep, First Look), you are paid once after the 30-day refund window closes.

Is there a minimum payout threshold?

No. If one investor subscribes to the Dashboard through your referral, you get €14.70 the next month. We do not hold your money hostage behind a threshold.

How long does attribution last?

For the life of the customer's account. If someone signs up through your referral code and stays for 3 years, you earn 30% every month for 3 years.

Can I refer other affiliates?

Not yet. This is a single-tier program. We may add a sub-affiliate tier if the program grows, but right now we want to keep it simple and transparent.

Do I need to be a paying customer to be an affiliate?

No. The Sunday Signal (free) is enough to understand the product and recommend it honestly. If you want the Dashboard to show investors what they get, start with the free tier and upgrade when it makes sense.

What if my referral asks for a refund?

Every paid tier has a 30-day money-back guarantee. If they refund, the commission on that sale is void. This is standard and fair.

Email us and we will agree your referral code the same day. No forms, no dashboard, no minimums.
