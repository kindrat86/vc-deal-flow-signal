Crunchbase vs Fundable: Different Tools for Different Buyers

Crunchbase and Fundable are not competitors: Crunchbase (from about $29 per user per month) is a startup intelligence database used by investors, sales teams, and researchers. Fundable ($179 per month flat) is a fundraising platform founders use to run equity or rewards raises from their own network. Pick Crunchbase to research the market, Fundable to raise money. Neither surfaces startups before their round is announced.

Quick verdict

If you are an investor, analyst, or founder doing market research, the answer is Crunchbase: it is a database of companies, funding rounds, investors, and news, queryable by sector, stage, and geography. If you are a founder actively raising from friends, family, and your network, the answer is Fundable: it packages your raise with a profile, payment handling, and back-office mechanics at one flat monthly price.

The people searching "Crunchbase vs Fundable" are usually mixing up two different purchases. Comparing them on features is like comparing a library catalog to a crowdfunding stall. The honest comparison is on cost, buyer, and what each one cannot do.

What each product actually is

Crunchbase is a market intelligence platform. Its core asset is structured data: company profiles, funding rounds with amounts and investors, acquirer and IPO data, news mentions, and contact data add-ons. Subscriptions run from a Starter tier around $29 per user per month, through Pro at about $49 per month billed annually (roughly $99 month-to-month), to Business at approximately $199 per month. Enterprise and API access is quoted custom. Buyers: investors, corporate development, sales and recruiting teams.

Fundable is a software-as-a-service fundraising platform. Founders build a company profile for free, then pay $179 per month to run a live raise, and Fundable charges no success fees on money collected (payment processing fees may still apply). Equity raises on Fundable target accredited investors and typically range from $50,000 to $10 million; rewards-based raises are also supported. Fundable is explicit that it is not a registered broker-dealer and takes no part in negotiating or executing investments. Buyers: founders.

Price comparison, side by side

  • Crunchbase: about $29/user/mo (Starter), $49/mo billed annually for Pro, ~$199/mo Business, custom for Enterprise/API. Contact-data add-ons extra.
  • Fundable: $0 to build a profile, then $179/mo flat while fundraising, no success fees, processing fees may apply. Pricing not pro-rated.
  • Entry cost difference: Crunchbase starts under $30/mo for one seat of read access. Fundable's real price is the raise itself: a three-month campaign costs $537 whether you collect commitments or not.

When to use which

Use Crunchbase when you need to answer "what exists" questions: which companies raised a Series A in climate tech last quarter, who backed them, what a competitor's footprint looks like. It is a research tool, and its value scales with how much you query it.

Use Fundable when you are a founder with a warm network and want infrastructure to collect commitments: a presentable profile, payment processing, and the mechanics of documenting an equity or rewards raise without a platform cut of the proceeds.

Use neither when your job is to find the next good startup before everyone else knows about it. Both tools are backward-looking: Crunchbase reports rounds after they are announced, and Fundable only shows you raises that founders themselves set up. Neither reads any leading indicator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Crunchbase or Fundable better for finding startups to invest in?

Crunchbase. It is a market intelligence database with funding rounds, valuations, and company profiles built for investors and researchers. Fundable is not a discovery database at all: it is a fundraising platform where founders run equity or rewards raises.

What does Fundable cost?

A flat $179 per month to run a fundraise, free to build a profile before launch, and no success fees on money collected. Payment processing fees may apply, and pricing is not pro-rated.

What does Crunchbase cost?

About $29 per user per month for Starter, roughly $49 per month billed annually for Pro (about $99 monthly), and approximately $199 per month for Business. Enterprise and API access is custom-priced.

Can investors use Fundable?

Only in a limited way. Equity raises are open to accredited investors, but the platform is built around a founder's own network, not around investor deal flow. Investors scouting new deals get nothing out of it.
