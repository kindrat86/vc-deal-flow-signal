How to Evaluate Startup Traction Without Revenue

Framework for assessing pre-revenue startup traction using non-financial signals.

Pre-revenue startups are hard to evaluate. But traction is not just revenue. Here are the signals that indicate a startup is gaining real momentum, even before they monetize.

Technical Traction Signals

  • GitHub star growth rate (week-over-week acceleration)
  • Contributor count and commit frequency
  • NPM/PyPI download growth for open-source projects
  • API usage and integration mentions

Market Traction Signals

  • Waitlist size and growth velocity
  • Community size (Discord, Slack, forum members)
  • Media mentions and organic search volume for the brand name
  • Partnership announcements

The Red Flags

Watch for: declining commit frequency after initial hype, no job postings for 3+ months, founder departure from public profiles, and pivots that abandon the original technical moat.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most reliable pre-revenue traction signal?

GitHub star growth acceleration combined with consistent commit activity is the most reliable technical signal. For non-technical startups, waitlist growth velocity and community engagement are strongest.
