The 3 Reasons You're Still on the Fence
(and why each one is wrong)

Every investor I've spoken to raises one of these three objections. Here's how each one actually holds up.

Reason #1 — The Vehicle Objection

"GitHub data is too noisy to be useful."

We don't show raw commits. We show acceleration patterns — when a company's engineering velocity deviates sharply from its own baseline. That's not noise, that's a regime change. And regime changes in engineering activity have historically preceded fundraise announcements by three to six weeks.

Reason #2 — The External Objection

"I already have enough deal flow."

Your network shows you what other investors already see. We show you what nobody else is watching. The deals that generate outsized returns are the ones where you arrive before consensus forms. If you're sourcing from Twitter + warm intros, you're sourcing the same pipeline as every other partner at the fund down the street.

Reason #3 — The Internal Objection

"I'm not a quant — I wouldn't know what to do with raw engineering data."

You won't see raw data. You'll see: "Company X. Healthcare. Seed. +180% commit acceleration. 94 new contributors. Flagged 6 weeks ago. Still accelerating." Then you reach out. If you've ever sent a cold intro email to a founder, you already have every skill this requires. The analysis is finished before the report hits your inbox.
