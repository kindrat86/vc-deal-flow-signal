    Frequently Asked Questions

    How is startup runway calculated?

    Runway = Cash on Hand ÷ Monthly Net Burn. If you have $500k in the bank and burn $40k/month net, your runway is 12.5 months. Net burn = gross monthly expenses minus monthly revenue.

    When should I start fundraising?

    Raise when you have 9-12 months of runway left. A typical seed/Series A round takes 3-6 months from first pitch to wired cash, and investors get nervous when founders are visibly desperate. Starting at 12 months gives you leverage to walk away from bad terms.

    What's a "good" runway?

    Post-fund-raise, VCs expect 18-24 months of runway, enough to hit the milestones that justify the next round. Below 12 months is a yellow flag. Below 6 months is a crisis: you have weeks to close or cut burn hard.
