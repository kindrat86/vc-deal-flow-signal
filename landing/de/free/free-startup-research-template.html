FAQ

How long should due diligence take?

Pre-seed: 5-10 hours over 1-2 weeks. Seed: 20-40 hours over 3-4 weeks. Series A: 60-100 hours over 4-6 weeks. Use the template to stay organized.

Should I hire a due diligence firm?

For checks under $100K, no. The template plus your network is enough. For checks over $500K, consider specialized DD firms for tech, financial, and legal diligence.

Can the template be used for non-tech startups?

Partially. The founder and market sections apply to any startup. The technology section is tech-focused — adapt for consumer, services, or healthcare startups.
