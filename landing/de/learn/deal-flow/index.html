What Is Deal Flow in Venture Capital?

Understanding deal flow: how VCs source, evaluate, and track startup investment opportunities.

Deal flow is the lifeblood of venture capital. It refers to the stream of investment opportunities that a VC firm evaluates. The quality and quantity of your deal flow determines your returns.

Deal Flow Sources

  • Inbound: founders pitching you directly
  • Network: referrals from other investors and founders
  • Outbound: your team actively sourcing deals
  • Signal-based: using data to identify companies before they raise

Why Signal-Based Deal Flow Wins

Traditional deal flow relies on who you know. Signal-based deal flow relies on what the data tells you. The best investors use both, but signal-based sourcing gives you access to deals that never enter the referral network.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good deal flow for a seed-stage VC?

A typical seed-stage VC evaluates 500-1000 companies per year and invests in 5-15. The goal is not more deal flow, but better filtering of the flow you have.
