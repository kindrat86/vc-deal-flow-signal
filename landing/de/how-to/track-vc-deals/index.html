How to Track VC Deals Using Public Data

Methods for tracking venture capital deals and valuations using publicly available data.

You do not need a $20,000 PitchBook subscription to track VC deals. Much of the data is available publicly if you know where to look.

Free Deal Tracking Sources

  • SEC Form D filings (mandatory for US fundraising)
  • Crunchbase free tier (announced rounds only)
  • TechCrunch and sector press
  • Accelerator demo day announcements
  • Companies House (UK) and equivalent registries

Building a Deal Flow Dashboard

Combine these sources with GitDealFlow's signal tracking to build a complete picture. The free sources tell you what already happened. GitDealFlow tells you what is about to happen.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are SEC Form D filings public?

Yes. Form D filings are public records filed with the SEC whenever a company raises money under Regulation D exemptions. They include the amount raised and the date, though not the valuation.
