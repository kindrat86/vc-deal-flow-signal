GitDealFlow + Airtable

Airtable is the most flexible database for startup tracking. Combine it with GitDealFlow's engineering momentum data to build custom deal flow dashboards, automated scoring systems, and collaborative pipeline views.

How the Integration Works

Weekly export your GitDealFlow watchlist as CSV. Import into an Airtable base with columns for: startup name, sector, stage, momentum score, commit velocity, contributor count, last contact date, next step, and outcome. Use Airtable's views (grid, kanban, calendar) and automations (Slack alerts on score changes) to operationalize the data.

Advanced: use Airtable Scripting or Make/Zapier to auto-import GitDealFlow data weekly. Set up conditional formatting to highlight startups with momentum scores above your investment threshold.
