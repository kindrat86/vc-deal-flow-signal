MarTech Startups — Funding Signals & Deal Flow

Marketing automation, cdp, and growth-tooling software — tracked at the GitHub-org level across ~400 startups.

GitDealFlow tracks marketing automation, CDP, and growth-tooling software startup momentum derived from public GitHub activity: commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository expansion. We surface breakout martech engineering teams 3–6 weeks before their fundraise is announced — early enough to matter, late enough to be real. The martech sector bucket includes CDP and customer data infrastructure; marketing automation platforms; attribution and analytics; personalization engines; marketing-integration connectors.

MarTech sector overview

MarTech momentum shows up as CDP platform commits, attribution-engine work, and integration-connector repo growth. GitDealFlow weights integration-connector creation highest — it correlates with paid customer onboarding.

The sector covers approximately 5 active sub-focus areas. Teams that ship weekly commits across multiple sub-areas are the strongest predictor of near-term commercial traction; single-repo teams are typically earlier-stage and noisier.

Recent martech funding trends

CDP and attribution infrastructure have led martech GitHub momentum in 2026. Teams adding new marketing-platform connectors weekly are typically 3–5 weeks from a raise.

The pattern repeats across sectors: engineering acceleration in production-deployment repos (serving, integration, SDK) precedes fundraise announcements by 3–6 weeks. Research-output acceleration alone is a weaker signal — it correlates with academic output, not commercial traction.

Top martech signals to track

The GitDealFlow methodology weights the following signals most heavily when scoring martech startup momentum:

  • Marketing-connector creation rate
  • CDP platform commit velocity
  • Attribution-engine contributor growth
  • Personalization releases
How to verify a martech signal: pull the org's last 12 weeks of commits via GET https://signals.gitdealflow.com/api/signals.json?sector=martech, or install the MCP server with npx -y @gitdealflow/mcp-signal and call search_startups_by_sector("martech").
