The Problem: Competitors Ship in Silence

By the time a competitor announces a new product, hires a famous executive, or raises a round, the interesting part has already happened. Press releases are the last place intelligence appears, not the first. The leading indicators — a team expanding, a codebase growing, a new repository appearing — are visible in public GitHub activity weeks earlier, but only if someone is systematically watching.

GitDealFlow makes that watching automatic. It reads the public GitHub activity of 350+ startups and tracks commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository expansion. The same signals that precede fundraising by 21-47 days (a pattern documented in a research panel of 219 fundraises) also reveal what a competitor is building, how fast, and with how many people — before any announcement.

What Competitive Intelligence Reveals

Product direction — what repos are they creating? New repositories indicate new products or major initiatives; sustained work on existing ones indicates deepening or maintenance. The repository expansion signal makes this visible at a glance.

Engineering velocity — are they shipping fast or plateauing? Commit velocity trends show whether a team is accelerating into a launch or slowing into maintenance mode. Velocity is the closest public proxy for execution speed.

Hiring signals — contributor growth indicates team expansion. When new contributors appear and stay, a company is scaling its engineering organization — often ahead of a product push or a fundraise.

Tech stack changes — are they adopting new technologies? New languages, frameworks, and tooling in public repositories signal pivots, platform investments, or new product lines before they are announced.

A Simple Weekly Workflow

  • Subscribe to the free digest and add your competitor set to your watchlist.
  • Review the five names each Sunday and check any competitor that appears.
  • Open the company's GitHub organization and verify: new repos, commit cadence, contributor count.
  • Note material changes in your competitive log — direction shifts, hiring waves, velocity spikes.
  • Act on the signal: adjust your roadmap, pricing, or outreach before the competitor's announcement lands.

Every signal is computed from public data you can verify yourself, so competitive claims in your internal briefings always have a source you can open in one click.
