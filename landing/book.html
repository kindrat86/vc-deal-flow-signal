What the book covers

The seven signals, in the order the book teaches them:

  • Commit velocity: sustained rises in weekly commits, the backbone signal.
  • Contributor influx: new engineering hires appearing in public repos before any announcement.
  • Star detachment: star growth decoupling from day-to-day engineering activity.
  • Infra buildout: CI, deployment and tooling repositories multiplying ahead of a raise.
  • Issue cadence: how fast a team closes its own backlog, a proxy for shipping discipline.
  • Founding-team visibility: founders still committing code, a signal of pre-scale teams.
  • Dependency adoption: fast integration of new infrastructure libraries by young teams.

Every chapter pairs the signal with the correlation evidence, the failure modes, and a worked checklist. The replication appendix re-derives each finding from the public dataset so you can check the work yourself.
