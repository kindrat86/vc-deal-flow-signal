That game lives on the signal board at signals.gitdealflow.com/predict during the merge. The signal underneath it is the same one this site publishes weekly.

How to read a breakout signal yourself

  • Commit velocity: look for sustained multi-week rises, not single spikes; releases and hackathons spike, hiring curves ramp.
  • Contributor growth: new regular contributors appearing in the org's repos is the earliest hiring signal that precedes an announcement.
  • Repository expansion: new infrastructure and tooling repos multiplying means a team is building for scale it has not announced.

The evidence behind the signal

The method is published, not proprietary folklore. The GitDealFlow panel tracks 350+ venture-backed startup GitHub organizations across 15 sectors weekly, and the methodology paper is SSRN-indexed with a public replication appendix, so every threshold in the book can be re-derived from the dataset. Breakout teams in the panel surface 21 to 47 days before their round is announced; that window is what the weekly digest mails to investors every Sunday.

Honest limits, because prediction pages are usually dishonest: momentum signals miss hardware and biotech teams whose engineering happens outside public GitHub, they fire late on stealth companies that ship privately, and an open-core project can look hot without a business behind it. Treat every score as a reason to investigate, never as an outcome.
