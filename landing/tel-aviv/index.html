GitDealFlow for Tel Aviv

Built for investors sourcing in Tel Aviv. See which startups are heating up before they raise.

Tel Aviv is one of the most active startup ecosystems in the world per capita, a dense cluster of deep-tech, cybersecurity, fintech, and enterprise software companies, with a funding market that moves fast. For investors sourcing here, the problem is not a lack of startups; it is the speed at which rounds get oversubscribed once they are announced. By the time a Tel Aviv startup appears in the funding news, the best entry is usually gone.

That is where the signal comes in. GitDealFlow reads public GitHub activity across 350+ startups, commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository expansion, and flags the teams that are accelerating, typically 21-47 days before their round hits the press. In a market like Tel Aviv, where engineering quality is the core export, public engineering activity is a remarkably direct proxy for what is being built: teams hire, ship, and expand in public before they raise in public.

What you get

  • A free weekly email with five startup names, every Sunday, one-click unsubscribe, no spam.
  • Engineering momentum data you can verify: the methodology is published on SSRN under CC BY 4.0 with an open dataset.
  • Free programmatic access for your own pipeline: JSON API, CSV export, and an MCP server at signals.gitdealflow.com.
  • Paid tiers (Dashboard and Insider) for deeper tracking and automation.

How investors here use it

Angels use the weekly list as a first screen, then diligence the names with local context, the neighborhood, the unit, the network. Scouts pass accelerating teams to their funds before the round opens. Seed funds watch the pipeline for engineering acceleration in their thesis sectors. The common thread: the signal tells you where to look weeks before everyone else is looking.

Why This Page Exists

GitDealFlow is a public deal flow signal dataset: 350+ startup GitHub organizations across 15 sectors, refreshed weekly, with breakout teams surfacing 21 to 47 days before their round is announced. This page makes one part of that system legible: what it measures, how it is computed, and how to use it in a live sourcing workflow. The method is published end to end and falsifiable by design, with the working paper on SSRN and the dataset downloadable under CC BY 4.0.
