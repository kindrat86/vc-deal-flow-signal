Average Seed Round Size in 2026: Use the Median, Not the Headline

The median US seed round in 2026 is about $3 million to $4 million raised on a median post-money valuation that hit a record $24 million in late 2025, per Carta's benchmarks. The mathematical average runs higher, near $5.6 million, because a small number of outlier AI rounds pull it up. This page consolidates the stage-by-stage numbers from Carta, PitchBook-NVCA, and AngelList reporting so you can benchmark a raise or a deal without repeating the average-vs-median confusion.

The headline numbers

  • Median seed round (US): ~$3.1M all-industry aggregate; Carta's priced-seed benchmark puts it at ~$4.1M for its panel (H2 2025 window, published July 2026).
  • Average seed round: ~$5.6M, inflated by billion-dollar outlier rounds. Never plan against an average.
  • Median seed post-money: $24M-$24.3M (Carta, Q4 2025), up from ~$18M in Q4 2024, an increase driven largely by AI deals.
  • Median seed dilution: 18-20%, with 19.5% the Carta figure most cited.
  • Top-decile seed: consensus pre-money near $40M (PitchBook, Q4 2025), roughly 3x the market median; tier-one-led rounds of $5M-$10M.

Why the average misleads

Seed round sizes are heavily right-skewed: most rounds cluster between $1.5M and $5M, while a handful of AI mega-rounds land at $20M or more. A single fund's headline "average seed round" can double on one outlier. PitchBook-NVCA's Q1 2026 Venture Monitor illustrates the fragility: removing just five deals cut headline deal value by 73%. Medians resist that distortion, which is why every benchmark on this page leads with the median and treats the average as a curiosity.

Seed in context: the full ladder

To see how seed sits in the funding ladder, the neighboring stages on the same datasets:

  • Pre-seed: $750K-$1.5M raised (median ~$1M), typically on SAFEs with caps of $10M-$15M rather than priced equity.
  • Seed: $2M-$5M typical band, median $3M-$4M, record $24M median post-money.
  • Series A: median $19.6M deal at $78.7M post-money (PitchBook-NVCA, Q1 2026); non-AI rounds price closer to $40-$42M pre-money.
  • Series B: ~$40M median deal on $120M-$160M+ post-money.

The pattern investors should internalize: round sizes have stayed comparatively flat while valuations, especially for AI companies, have moved sharply. Seed is not bigger in 2026; it is more expensive.

Sector and geography splits that matter

Industry changes the answer materially. Aggregated 2026 deal data puts AI and healthcare seed rounds at $4M-$5M while consumer apps run $1.5M-$2.5M. More than 60% of capital Carta recorded in Q1 2026 went to AI companies, and AI/ML startups took 41.5% of AngelList deal share in H1 2025. Geography moves it just as much: European seed checks from new funds typically run €500K-€2.5M, Africa's median seed rose to ~$1.6M in 2025, and Latin American seed rounds generally sit below the US median.
