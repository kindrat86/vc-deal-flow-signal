# Learn

If you are new to early-stage investing, the vocabulary can feel like a wall: deal flow, pre-seed, SAFEs, valuation caps, signal versus noise. The guides below explain the fundamentals in plain language, and show where engineering momentum data fits into a modern evaluation process.

Every guide on this page builds on the same observation that powers GitDealFlow: public GitHub activity is a leading indicator. Before a startup raises, before it hires a banker, before the round hits the press, its engineering team usually starts moving faster. GitDealFlow reads the public GitHub activity of 350+ startups and tracks commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository expansion to spot that acceleration early.

## Fundamentals

### [What Is Deal Flow in Venture Capital?](https://gitdealflow.com/learn/deal-flow)

Deal flow is the stream of investment opportunities a fund or investor evaluates, and its quality determines portfolio quality. How VCs source, evaluate, and track opportunities, and where engineering momentum fits in a modern sourcing workflow.

### [What Is Pre-Seed Investing?](https://gitdealflow.com/learn/pre-seed-investing)

The stage before the first institutional round, when startups have the least data and the most risk. Definitions, deal structures, and evaluating opportunities when revenue is thin or nonexistent.

### [How to Build an Investment Thesis](https://gitdealflow.com/learn/how-to-build-an-investment-thesis)

A thesis is a falsifiable claim about where returns will come from. How to write one, test it against data, and let it discipline your sourcing instead of rationalizing your deals.

### [How to Structure a Syndicate Deal](https://gitdealflow.com/learn/how-to-structure-a-syndicate)

Carry, SPVs, and deal-by-deal economics for angels who want to write bigger checks together without starting a fund.

### [What Is a Deal Flow Signal?](https://gitdealflow.com/learn/what-is-a-deal-flow-signal)

What separates a tradeable leading indicator from lagging coverage, and the anatomy of the three GitHub signals GitDealFlow tracks.
