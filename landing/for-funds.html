Fair questions

How is this different from the GitDealFlow Dashboard?

The Dashboard is self-serve: you pick sectors and watch the board yourself. For-funds is done-for-you: you give us your written thesis and we hand-pick and explain the matches from the full 350+ org signal set, in a monthly report.

What data is this built on?

Public GitHub engineering activity across 350+ startup organizations: commit and contributor momentum, engineering hiring bursts, and velocity relative to sector benchmarks. No private data, no code reading, no scraping of private repos.

Is this investment advice?

No. It is research signal to investigate, not a recommendation to invest. Every match comes with the reason it surfaced so your team can judge it.

What happens after we pay?

You get a welcome email asking for your thesis (or send it first via the free sample request). The first monthly report lands within 10 business days of receiving your thesis. The 30-day Signal-or-It's-Free guarantee applies from your first payment.

Can we try it before paying?

Yes, and we prefer it. Request a free sample report with your thesis and we will send one matched sample so you can judge the signal before any money moves.
