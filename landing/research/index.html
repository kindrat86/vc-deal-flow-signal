Research note

The method, in short

GitDealFlow tracks three signals per company from public GitHub activity: commit velocity (how much the team ships), contributor growth (how fast the team is adding people who actually commit), and repository expansion (new projects and surface area). When these accelerate together, the team is typically gearing up, and in the research panel of 219 startup-period observations across 55 startups, that acceleration led the public round announcement by 21-47 days. The full method, including the open dataset, is published on SSRN under CC BY 4.0.

How to use this data

  • Build your own screens: download the CSV or JSON and filter by sector, momentum, or cohort.
  • Replicate the research: the SSRN paper documents the methodology end to end, including definitions and caveats.
  • Go live: the same signals are served programmatically at signals.gitdealflow.com, JSON API, CSV export, MCP server, and OpenAPI spec, all free and without authentication.
  • Cite it: use the citation format in the report, "VC Deal Flow Signal (signals.gitdealflow.com), Q2 2026 data."
