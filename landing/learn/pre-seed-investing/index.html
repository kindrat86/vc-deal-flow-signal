What Is Pre-Seed Investing?

A guide to the pre-seed stage: definitions, deal structures, and how to evaluate opportunities.

Pre-seed is the earliest stage of venture investment. Companies at this stage typically have a prototype, early users, or a strong founding team, but little to no revenue.

Pre-Seed Deal Characteristics

  • Check sizes: $100K to $2M
  • Valuations: $2M to $15M (pre-money)
  • Structure: SAFE, convertible note, or priced equity
  • Traction: prototype, waitlist, early users, or strong team

How to Evaluate Pre-Seed Deals

At this stage, you are investing in the team and the market, not the product. Look for founders with deep domain expertise, a large addressable market, and early technical or user signals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between pre-seed and seed?

Pre-seed rounds are typically $100K-$2M for building the product and team. Seed rounds are $2M-$10M for go-to-market after initial traction.
