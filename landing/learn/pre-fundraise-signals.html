TL;DR: The engineering signals that precede startup fundraise announcements by 3-6 weeks.

The 3-6 week window

Startups typically accelerate engineering 3-6 weeks before announcing a raise. This is when they staff up and push harder.

Contributor growth as a signal

A sudden jump in unique contributors often precedes a fundraise — new hires ship code.

Sector rotation

When momentum shifts across multiple startups in the same sector, it often signals a broader trend or capital cycle.
