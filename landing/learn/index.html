Guides

What Is Deal Flow in Venture Capital?

Deal flow is the stream of investment opportunities a fund or investor evaluates, and its quality determines portfolio quality. This guide explains how VCs source, evaluate, and track opportunities, and where new signal sources like engineering momentum fit into a modern sourcing workflow alongside networks and inbound referrals.

What Is Pre-Seed Investing?

The pre-seed stage sits before the first institutional round, when startups have the least data and the most risk. This guide covers the stage's definitions, typical deal structures, and how to evaluate opportunities when revenue is thin or nonexistent, including non-financial signals such as commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository expansion.

What Is Commit Velocity?

The engineering metric investors overlook: what commit velocity measures, how to read its trend, and why it rises 3 to 6 weeks before a fundraise. Includes the formula, the limitations, and how to combine it with the other two signals.

What Is Engineering Velocity?

The broader signal built from commit velocity, contributor growth, and repository expansion, and why it leads funding news by weeks. Includes the difference between engineering velocity and product velocity.

What Is Contributor Growth?

Why a growing team of active committers is conviction made visible, and how to read team expansion as a buy signal. Includes the red flags: single-bus-factor teams, contractor churn, and vanity open-source.

Venture Scouting: Best Practices

The four practices that separate productive scouts from busy ones: sourcing from leading indicators, a repeatable weekly routine, writing receipts, and optimizing for access and speed.

GitHub Signals for VC Due Diligence

How to use public GitHub evidence to verify execution, team scaling, and product direction during due diligence, and what the three signals can and cannot prove.

Engineering Benchmarks by Funding Stage

What engineering maturity looks like from pre-seed to Series B, and why stage and sector context beat any universal benchmark number.
