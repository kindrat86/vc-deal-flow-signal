GitDealFlow for Slack, Get Deal Flow Alerts in Your Team Channel

What Slack Integration Delivers

Weekly digest: 'Top 10 trending startups this week' posted to your #deal-flow channel.

Momentum alerts: Get pinged when a startup you're tracking has a significant velocity change.

Scout Score updates: Weekly list of GitHub users with rising Scout Scores.

The Slack integration puts the GitDealFlow signal where your team already works. Instead of a newsletter that sits in an inbox, the weekly list lands in the channel your fund uses for deal discussion, so sourcing becomes a team conversation instead of a solo habit. Momentum alerts arrive when they matter, and Scout Score updates give the whole group a shared view of whose radar is early.

How the Integration Works

  • Delivery: the integration posts to your chosen channel via webhook, no Slack app install or OAuth scopes required for the core digest.
  • Weekly digest: every Sunday, the five-name signal list plus the broader trending board is posted automatically.
  • Momentum alerts: changes in commit velocity, contributor growth, or repository expansion for startups on your watchlist trigger a targeted message.
  • Scout Score updates: a weekly rundown of GitHub users whose starring history shows them early on breakout companies.
  • Insider Circle: members get automated Slack delivery configured for them, including custom channel routing.

Who Benefits

Fund teams keep everyone aligned on a shared pipeline: analysts see the same names as partners, and the channel history becomes a sourcing log. Scouts use momentum alerts to move fast on a startup they were already tracking. Solo angels use Slack as a lightweight inbox, the digest arrives, they skim it, and the five names are in their workflow without another tool to check. All of it runs on public GitHub data, so there is nothing to configure on the startup side.

Why Native Integrations Matter

Deal flow tooling only works if it lives where the work happens. GitDealFlow publishes machine-readable surfaces so research lands inside existing stacks: a Model Context Protocol server, an A2A endpoint, an NLWeb endpoint, and a JSON API covering 350+ organizations in 15 sectors. When an analyst asks for breakout startups, the answer can arrive as live data in the tool they already use, instead of a stale export pasted between systems.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up the Slack integration?

Via webhook. Insider Circle members get automated Slack delivery.

Can I route different signals to different channels?

Yes, Insider Circle members can configure separate channels for the weekly digest, momentum alerts, and Scout Score updates.

What data flows through the integration?

Only public GitHub-derived signals: startup names, momentum scores, commit and contributor trends, sector tags, and Scout Scores. No private data is involved.

How do I stop the alerts?

Delete the webhook from your Slack channel settings and the integration stops immediately. The email signal, if you have it, unsubscribes with one click.
