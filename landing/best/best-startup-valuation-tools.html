The Alternatives, Reviewed

Carta

Carta is the leading cap table and equity management platform, with 409A valuations, portfolio tracking, and fund administration. It is a tool for managing the companies you have already invested in, not for finding new ones.

Best for: Cap tables, 409A valuations, equity management. Pricing: from $280/yr Launch (scales with stakeholders). Choose it when you need cap tables and 409A valuations for a company you already hold.

PrivCo

PrivCo is a financial-intelligence database focused on US private companies, with proprietary revenue, EBITDA, and valuation estimates across 5M+ companies, most of them bootstrapped or family-owned. It is built for M&A and diligence rather than early-stage deal sourcing.

Best for: US private company financials, M&A, valuations. Pricing: subscription, free limited account plus paid tiers. Choose it when you need US private-company revenue and valuation estimates for diligence.

Grata

Grata is a middle-market private company intelligence platform, with revenue estimates, EBITDA figures, ownership structures, and 800,000+ transactions for comparable analysis. It is built for M&A and buyout professionals rather than early-stage venture.

Best for: Middle-market sourcing, M&A, revenue estimates. Pricing: custom, enterprise. Choose it when you are sourcing middle-market M&A targets.

PitchBook

PitchBook is the institutional standard for private capital market data: 3.4M+ companies, 3,000+ research analysts, and deep PE/VC financials. It is the gold standard for post-announcement financial data, cap tables, and LP reporting, and it is priced for funds with a dedicated research budget.

Best for: Institutional financial data, LP reporting, PE/M&A. Pricing: custom, roughly $20,000-$30,000/yr per seat. Choose it when you need cap tables, valuation data, and LP-grade reporting for a term sheet or fund.

How to Choose

Before you compare features, weigh each tool against the criteria that actually decide whether it earns its cost:

  • Cap table: Does it model ownership and dilution.
  • Comps: Does it give you comparable transactions.
  • Financial estimates: Revenue and EBITDA where available.
  • Momentum: Does it tell you whether growth is accelerating.
  • Cost: From startup pricing to enterprise.
