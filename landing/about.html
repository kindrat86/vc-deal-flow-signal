About GitDealFlow

The signal behind the Velocity Verdict

You do not need another startup database. You need a signal you can trust earlier.

In short: you can trust this signal earlier because none of it asks for your trust. The ranking method — called Code-Side Sourcing (published in full at signals.gitdealflow.com/code-side-sourcing) — is the practice of using public repository-velocity data as a leading indicator of venture-stage outcomes, surfacing fundraises 21 to 47 days before pitch decks circulate.

The dataset is CC BY 4.0 and downloadable, and the metric it ranks by — change in 14-day GitHub commit velocity — has historically preceded fundraise announcements by three to six weeks.

VC Deal Flow Signal exists because most tools get stronger after the story is already public. This product was built around a different question: what changed before the round starts feeling obvious, and how can you check that without taking a vendor's word for it?
