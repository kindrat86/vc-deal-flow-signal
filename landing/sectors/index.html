Why This Page Exists

GitDealFlow is a public deal flow signal dataset: 400+ startup GitHub organizations across 15 sectors, refreshed weekly, with breakout teams surfacing 21 to 47 days before their round is announced. This page makes one part of that system legible: what it measures, how it is computed, and how to use it in a live sourcing workflow. The method is published end to end and falsifiable by design, with the working paper on SSRN and the dataset downloadable under CC BY 4.0.
