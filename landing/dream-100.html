Investor Newsletters & Publications

The inboxes that set the agenda before the Monday pipeline meeting. These eight publications reach the investors who source before the crowd.

Term Sheet (Fortune)

The daily VC/PE briefing from Fortune. Covers deals, fundraises, and the personalities behind them. Essential reading for institutional investors.

fortune.com/tag/term-sheet

Axios Pro Rata

Dan Primack's daily dealmaker briefing on VC, PE, and M&A. Concise, well-sourced, and widely forwarded inside every major fund.

axios.com/newsletters/axios-pro-rata

StrictlyVC

Connie Loizos's daily newsletter covering the VC scene in Silicon Valley and beyond. Now part of Yahoo/TechCrunch, with intimate in-person events.

The news arm of the PitchBook data platform. Covers private market trends, fund performance, and sector analysis with institutional data backing.

pitchbook.com/news

Sifted

Europe's leading startup and VC publication. Covers fundraising, fintech, deeptech, and the European ecosystem with deep analytical reporting.

sifted.eu

TechCrunch

The original tech startup publication. Its venture category covers funding rounds, VC moves, and startup launches. A daily must-scan for anyone in the ecosystem.

techcrunch.com/category/venture

The Information

Jessica Lessin's subscription publication for deep tech and finance reporting. Widely read by VCs, founders, and executives who need the story behind the story.

theinformation.com

CB Insights Newsletter

Data-driven research on tech markets, startups, and venture capital. Known for sharp analysis, market maps, and the AI 100 list. Read by analysts and investors.

cbinsights.com/newsletter

podcast.thepitch.vc

How great companies scale from zero to global. Reid Hoffman interviews founders and CEOs about the inflection points that defined their growth.
