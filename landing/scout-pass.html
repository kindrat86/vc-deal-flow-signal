Questions scouts actually ask

Is this really free? What's the catch?

It's free for active scouts. No catch, no credit card. Scouts are the most important distribution channel in early-stage VC, if GitDealFlow is useful to you, you'll tell your partners. When you eventually join or start a fund, you'll upgrade to Insider Circle. That's the model.

Who qualifies as a "scout"?

Anyone who sources deals for a VC fund or angel invests actively: formal scout program members, informal fund scouts, and independent angels with 2+ disclosed investments in the past year.

How do you verify I'm a real scout?

LinkedIn profile + program affiliation, checked by hand. Independent angels: AngelList/Crunchbase investment history. The whole process takes 24-48 hours for most applicants.

Can I keep my Scout Pass forever?

It's a 12-month renewable term. As long as you're actively scouting or angel investing, renew indefinitely. There's an annual check-in.

Can I share signals with my fund partners?

Yes, that's the point. Forward the digest, screenshot signals, bring names to partner meetings. Just don't resell the data as a competing product.

I'm a student interested in VC. Do I qualify?

Not for Scout Pass, but the free Sunday email at gitdealflow.com is perfect for learning deal-flow signals. When you start scouting or investing, come back and apply.
