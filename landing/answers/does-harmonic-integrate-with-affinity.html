Does Harmonic Integrate with Affinity?

Yes. Harmonic.ai ships a native Affinity integration, and it syncs both ways. New companies Harmonic discovers can be pushed straight into Affinity as records, and the firms that run it report a reverse-sync that enriches Harmonic with the relationship context their partners are already tracking.

The short answer

Yes, Harmonic integrates natively with Affinity. Harmonic's own documentation lists a native Affinity integration alongside its other CRM connections, and a published case study from Valo Ventures describes the sync running in production. Harmonic pushes newly formed companies into the firm's Affinity workspace, and the firm set up a reverse-sync so enrichment flows back from Affinity into Harmonic. The result is a two-way loop: discovery happens in Harmonic and lands in the CRM, while relationship context from Affinity flows back to sharpen Harmonic's relevance scoring.

What the integration actually does

  • Harmonic to Affinity: newly founded or newly relevant companies surface as Affinity records, so partners see a fresh lead in the same place they track pipeline.
  • Affinity to Harmonic: a reverse-sync writes back relationship and pipeline context, which Harmonic uses to prioritize and re-score companies already on your radar.
  • Two-way relevance: Valo Ventures used the sync to triage long event attendee lists, prioritizing which companies to meet from the combined signal.

How to set it up

The Affinity connection is a native option inside Harmonic, not a Zapier workaround. If your firm runs a different CRM, Harmonic also connects to the major CRMs through Polytomic, a third-party data-sync layer. Affinity itself exposes a REST API and webhook triggers, so teams with in-house data tooling can build a direct pipeline as well. No separate public price is published for the integration; it is bundled with Harmonic's enterprise plan, sold as an annual contract.

What Harmonic and Affinity each do

Quick orientation, because the question mixes two tools that solve different problems. Harmonic is a startup discovery and team-pattern-matching platform: it surfaces companies by founder background, hiring signals, and headcount growth. Affinity is a relationship-intelligence CRM: it maps your firm's network, tracks pipeline, and surfaces warm-introduction paths. The integration exists because the two are complements, not substitutes. Harmonic finds companies you do not know yet, and Affinity manages the ones you do.
