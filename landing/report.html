What is in a GitDealFlow report?

Each entry shows four metrics read from public GitHub activity: momentum change, commits over the last 14 days, contributor count, and stage or geography. On this sample, the top entry moved +329% on 30 commits from 37 contributors.

How are the entries ranked?

Ranking follows engineering acceleration, not popularity. A sustained high-output team like the #2 entry here (267 commits in 14 days, +75%) can outrank a flashier one-week spike. The full method is public: signals.gitdealflow.com/methodology.
