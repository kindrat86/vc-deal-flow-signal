Common questions

Is this a one-time replacement for Insider Circle?

No. Insider Circle is the ongoing pulse. Sector Sweep is a one-time, custom-built, deep cut on your thesis. Many investors do both. If you do, the €1,997 credits toward your first ~20 months of Insider.

What sectors are in scope?

Anything where engineering activity is a meaningful signal. The 20 sectors we already track plus net-new ones we'll discover for you. The only sectors we decline are ones where GitHub activity isn't a signal (deep biotech with closed-source IP, hardware-only without firmware, regulated finance with proprietary trading).

Can I commission a sweep for a private portfolio review?

Yes. Send me a list of 10 to 30 orgs and I'll run them against the same signal stack, plus surface 10 to 20 net-new orgs that fit the same profile and are accelerating now. Same price.

Why anonymity? Why no live call?

I'm a solo bootstrapped operator. I keep the work and the founder identity separate. The trade-off is no live calls — you get an async written walkthrough that's actually more thorough than a 60-min call would be, and a 14-day email Q&A window. Several Sweep clients have said the written format is what they preferred anyway: re-readable, paste-able into their notes, no scheduling.

Why only 8 sweeps per quarter?

Because each one is custom and I want to keep the depth. Two sweeps a week is the sustainable cap for one operator. When the cap fills, the queue rolls to the next quarter.
