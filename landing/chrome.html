How does the extension work?

When you open a company profile on Crunchbase or Wellfound, the extension reads the company slug from the page URL, queries the public signal API at signals.gitdealflow.com, and renders a small badge inline showing whether that company's GitHub engineering activity is accelerating. If the company isn't in the tracked set, the badge says so — no guessing, no scores invented on the spot.

Which sites does it support?

Two, deliberately: Crunchbase organization profiles and Wellfound company profiles. Those are the pages investors already have open when a name crosses their desk. The content script is scoped to exactly those URL patterns in the manifest, so it stays inert everywhere else in your browser.

Does it cost anything or need an account?

No. The extension is free, has no paid tier of its own, and requires no sign-up. It is a distribution surface for the signal, not a product gate. The paid GitDealFlow tiers live on the pricing page and are entirely separate from the extension.

Privacy and methodology

No account. No telemetry. No cookies. The extension checks the page URL, queries the public GitDealFlow surface, and renders the signal inline.
