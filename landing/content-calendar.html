Earned distribution, not paid acquisition

Publish useful work. Measure the result.

This is GitDealFlow's operating calendar for public research, open data, and product education. A draft is not a placement. A placement is not a qualified visit. Each step gets its own evidence.

Operating rule: publish only after a claim check, a source check, and a channel-specific permission check. Paid ads are not the default strategy. Meta and LinkedIn pixels remain disabled.
